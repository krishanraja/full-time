## What passes

The show sounds like a person making an interesting football argument to one listener. Contractions, ordinary football language, varied sentence lengths and earned slang belong. A clear opinion needs a specific reason. A qualification belongs beside the claim it changes, once. Repeated methodology explanations and forced catchphrases fail the writing review.

Facts, numbers, names, scope and source disagreements still pass their existing checks. Expressive language cannot invent tactics, intent, eyewitness detail or season outcomes. The fixed alias Spurs is licensed only when Tottenham is a participating club in the evidence pack.

`src/lib/pundit/spoken-style.ts` is the executable writing brief used by the generator. Reporter spec version 2 carries the revised persona. The clarity judge also assesses prose as speech. The Reporter measured rate range is 175 to 210 words per minute; the working centre is near 192, the selected audition's approximate rate. Names and numbers must remain clear. Other personas retain their existing audio gates until their auditions establish their own ranges.

The Reporter reference uses the audition's contemporary conversational voice, speech model and placed delivery directions. The existing production narrator does not yet reproduce that reference: its configured voice and model ladder must be explicitly aligned and verified before the remake can be described as production-ready. Never silently fall back to a slower model or voice and call it the approved performance.

A loudness-range measurement is a warning signal, not an intonation or enjoyment detector. Automated editorial and audio success does not imply human listening approval. Record those outcomes separately. Listen to the entire candidate before selecting growth excerpts.

## Six distinct performances

| AI Pundit | Argument and delivery | Next proof |
|---|---|---|
| Reporter | Brisk, animated, opinionated, wry confidence; clear contrast rather than recital | Full-length remake using B as reference, then human listening verdict |
| Gaffer | Clipped and candid; a defensible call on recorded decisions, occasional incredulity | Short audition on a decision with sufficient evidence |
| Numbers Guy | Curious and animated; explain the surprising implication, slow locally for a difficult number | Short audition that makes a numerical contrast easy to follow |
| Romantic | Emotional rise and fall; earn the feeling through recorded moments, avoid constant poetry | Short audition with a genuine turning point |
| Doomer | Conversational deadpan; precise timing and a concrete failure mode, never sluggish recital | Short audition; flat affect can be intentional and must be judged as character |
| Wind-Up | Gleeful and teasing; a specific contradiction, punchline, then supporting fact | Short audition with a sting that survives without shouting |

Do not give every persona the Reporter's speed or argument. Audition the other five before full episodes. Existing locked fictional growth treatments have their own dialogue and casting approvals; this standard does not rewrite them or turn fictional comedy into product evidence.

## Bounded production sequence

1. Freeze a full script and performance text against the existing sealed match evidence. Run deterministic gates first, with no model calls.
2. Quote exact narration request characters, model-review scope, price estimate, maximum takes and stop condition. Approval for an audition is not a new full-show budget.
3. Review the changed script once against the editorial standards. If it fails, stop before narration and show the failure. No automatic writer or claim-laboratory run.
4. Narrate once with the selected reference voice/model/directions; verify the actual transcript, numbers, names, duration, loudness and clipping. Stop on failure, with no unapproved retry.
5. Present the full audio for human listening. Keep the current published edition recoverable. Publishing/replacing a live edition requires the exact approved operation and a persisted readback.
6. Only then hand growth the exact published transcript, audio version and timing. Growth reuses the audio and cannot advertise a stronger performance than the linked edition delivers.
