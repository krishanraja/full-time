# 07 - Marketing

- **Status:** Current pre-launch playbook
- **Owner:** Founder and marketing
- **Purpose:** Define the audience, argument, proof, copy, channels, and limits for marketing Full Time.
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-08-11

## The call

Market Full Time as an AI-native football experience, not as a cheaper podcast and not as an accountability product with audio attached.

> One real match. Six AI Pundits. Pick the brain you fancy.

The listener benefit is choice: one match becomes six complete, playful arguments. AI is the reason that choice exists. Checked facts and **Show me why** make the experience safe enough to trust.

## Behavioural objective

After seeing a Full Time message, a football fan should choose an AI Pundit and press play because they expect a useful, fun reading of the match that feels impossible to make as a normal human show.

During pre-launch, the alternative action is to join the launch note for one email when the release gates pass.

## Primary audience

The working audience is a time-poor football fan who already knows the score and wants a sharper way to talk about the match. Their current workaround is a mix of highlight clips, score apps, social posts, and repeated studio takes. The cost is noise and sameness, not a sourced monetary loss.

Secondary audiences are AI-curious fans, punditry skeptics, and social fans who enjoy comparing arguments. Partners belong in [`08-sales.md`](./08-sales.md), not consumer campaign copy.

## Message hierarchy

1. Six complete AI Pundit shows from one real match.
2. Pick the one you fancy and switch whenever you like.
3. Tap **Show me why** for the checked facts underneath a key claim.
4. Each edition has its own generated look, argument, humour, script, and performance.
5. The preview is free and pre-launch. Daily reliability and paid access are not available promises.

Accountability is a supporting trust point. Use “How did they do?” only when settled records exist. Do not lead campaigns with prediction metrics, receipts, betting-adjacent language, or an empty track-record route.

## Positioning against alternatives

| Alternative       | What it gives                           | Full Time difference                                                  |
| ----------------- | --------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Score app         | The result and event list               | Six interpretations and a playable argument                           |
| Human studio show | A fixed panel and long production cycle | Six on-demand AI Pundit editions from one evidence base               |
| Generic AI recap  | One compressed summary                  | Different theses, humour, scripts, performances, and proof boundaries |
| Social feed       | Endless reactions                       | One focused show with checked facts underneath                        |

Never claim that Full Time replaces watching football or makes human football media obsolete.

## Proof order

1. Show the six AI Pundits.
2. Show the same match producing different titles or takes.
3. Show the real player and safe switch.
4. Open one proof card.
5. Explain the evidence boundary in plain language.
6. Mention pre-launch status and the next action.

The code paths behind approved claims are listed in [`product-state.json`](./product-state.json). No audience, retention, preference, accuracy, coverage, or revenue figure is approved yet.

## Copy bank

Headlines:

- “One match. Six AI Pundits.”
- “Pick the brain you fancy.”
- “The score is only the start.”
- “Same facts. Six different shows.”
- “Football has enough hot takes. Pick a better one.”

Supporting lines:

- “Each AI Pundit makes a complete show from the same checked match facts.”
- “Tap Show me why when you want the fact underneath the claim.”
- “Fresh look, fresh argument, same real match.”
- “The data shows what happened, but not always why.”
- “All six AI Pundits are free during pre-launch.”

Calls to action:

- “Pick your AI Pundit”
- “Play today's show”
- “Compare the shows”
- “Show me why”
- “Join the launch note”

Avoid:

- the retired character label
- “AI-powered podcast”
- “Your personal pundit”
- “Big Five coverage”
- “Daily without fail”
- “Unbiased analysis”
- “Guaranteed predictions”
- “Your personal Gary Neville”
- “Game-changing football experience”

## Pre-launch channels

Rank channels by the evidence they can produce now:

1. founder-led product demonstrations and listening sessions;
2. short side-by-side AI Pundit comparisons;
3. clips or cards that pair one playful claim with one checked fact;
4. football and AI communities where transparent product feedback is welcome;
5. the launch note;
6. partner discovery with publishers, leagues, clubs, data providers, and distribution platforms.

The Reporter RSS feed remains a distribution seam, but the web player is the primary product. Do not market six podcast feeds or a standalone Feed tab.

Community participation must be transparent. No astroturfing, fake fans, copied comments, or automated match-thread spam.

## Content briefs agents may draft

An autonomous marketing agent may prepare, but not publish without exact approval:

- a 15-second comparison showing two AI Pundit takes on the same match;
- a “meet the six” card set using approved personality lines;
- a **Show me why** demonstration using a real approved proof card;
- a product update explaining a shipped behavior and its evidence boundary;
- a launch-note landing variant with one CTA;
- a founder demo script grounded in the current preview.

Every brief must name audience, desired action, source files, claim list, and unresolved proof.

## Current promise boundaries

Do not claim:

- Premier-League-only filtering until the Teams server response and UI enforce it;
- club-built playlists or personally generated shows;
- a completed quiet settled-only track-record route;
- public forecast performance;
- daily publication reliability;
- a public launch date;
- a paid offer or future price;
- use of club, league, broadcaster, player, or competition marks without rights.

## Launch gate

Broad public launch activity starts only after [`19-release-state.md`](./19-release-state.md) records one revision passing editorial, voice, rights, data, forecast, rehearsal, legal, accessibility, monitoring, rollback, and human gates.

Until then, say **pre-launch preview**, **private verification**, or **free while we prove the daily system**. Do not manufacture urgency.

## Asset acceptance

A marketing asset passes only when:

- the audience and one next action are obvious;
- the football benefit appears before pipeline detail;
- AI Pundit terminology is exact;
- primary copy works at a ten-year-old reading level;
- each material claim has a current source;
- no line promises a known gap or disabled offer;
- the visual has no awkward wrap at 320, 393, tablet, or desktop widths;
- the asset remains a draft until the exact publication action is approved.

See [`21-go-to-market-agent.md`](./21-go-to-market-agent.md) for the autonomous workflow and objection bank.
